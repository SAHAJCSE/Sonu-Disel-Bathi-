## About the Business

**Sonu Diesel Bhatti** specializes in manufacturing and supplying different types and sizes of bhattis and burners.

This project brings traditional business operations into a centralized digital platform, making product information and inventory management easier to organize across multiple locations.

## Project Overview

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Product Catalogue | Present products and their details |
| Inventory Management | Organize products and stock records |
| Multi-location Management | Manage inventory across business locations |
| Admin Dashboard | Provide authorized administrators with management tools |

## Project Goals

- Digitize traditional business operations.
- Reduce manual record-keeping.
- Centralize product and inventory information.
- Improve visibility across business locations.
- Build a simple, efficient management experience.
